AI model (optional)

Void works on its own: its built-in analyst searches, reads and writes cited reports with no AI key. To get fluent narrative summaries and follow-up chat, optionally connect OpenAI (ChatGPT API), Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or any OpenAI-compatible server.

These settings are locked by the server administrator.

Privacy: with a cloud provider, the text of the pages Void reads (after redaction of emails, cards and passwords) is sent to that provider. Use a local server (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) if that is not acceptable.
